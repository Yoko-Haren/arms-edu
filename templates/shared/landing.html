Inside ARMS

One system. A workspace for each role.

Everyone signs in to a portal made for their job, and sees only what that job needs.

Upload the class record. ARMS does the checking.

  • Upload DepEd's e-class record, or a CSV, for each class.
  • Every grade is recomputed from the scores before you submit.
  • Advisers get their section's forms and SF9 cards in one place.
  • Give timed quizzes through a link. Learners never see the answer key.

Validate every class with confidence.

  • Upload the SF1 to enroll learners, and import teachers from SF7.
  • See each class at a glance: submitted, still encoding or returned.
  • Automatic checks flag mismatches and big jumps before you validate.
  • Lock a period and the next one opens by itself.

See the whole school, and what to do next.

  • Proficiency levels by subject, discipline and grade level.
  • School Performance compares this year with the last.
  • Honors Outlook shows who is on track for an award.
  • Choose the grading rules and open each school year.

Screens from the ARMS demo school. Every name in them is sample data.

Beyond the paperwork

Know where learners stand before the term ends.

Grades stop being a file in a folder. The same records show the school head what is happening, what is likely next, and where to start.

  • A forecast for every grade level

    A machine learning model projects next period's averages, tested on periods it had not seen.

  • Poro AI recommends

    One decision for each proficiency level, aimed at the grade component that scores lowest.

  • Honors Outlook

    Who is on track, who is one point short, and which grade is holding a learner back.

How a grade travels

From class record to report card, in five steps.

  1. Upload

    The teacher uploads the class record.

  2. Check

    ARMS recomputes every grade from the scores.

  3. Validate

    The registrar validates, or returns it with a note.

  4. Lock

    The period closes, and the next one opens.

  5. Print

    Forms and report cards are ready to download.

Checked before it is saved

Every upload is read and shown for review first. A file with a problem changes nothing.

Only what each role needs

Teachers, the registrar and school heads each see their own work.

Our partner school

Built with a real school, in Panabo City.

Northern Paramedical & Technological College of Panabo, Inc. has served learners and families since 1995. ARMS was built in partnership with NPTCI, so its teachers, registrar and school leaders can keep every academic record accurate and ready.
